Prevention of cardiovascular disease.

Cardiovascular disease remains the main cause of mortality in Europe and in the world. High blood pressure, elevated cholesterol, diabetes, obesity, and smoking are the most important risk factors. Doctors note that the development of heart disease depends on lifestyle and physical activity.

A new study analyzed the health of 2000 patients during 10 years. The data show that a healthy diet and regular exercise reduce the risk of a heart attack by 30 percent. Patients who received preventive therapy had fewer complications and better survival.

Modern medicine offers effective drugs for the treatment of hypertension. The dose of the medication is selected for each patient individually. Clinical examination and blood tests help the physician to diagnose the disease at an early stage.

However, prevention remains more effective than treatment. Public health programs should explain the risk factors to the population. The immune system and the nervous system also influence the course of the disease, and further research will examine these mechanisms.

The results of the study were published in a medical journal. The authors recommend regular screening for all patients.
